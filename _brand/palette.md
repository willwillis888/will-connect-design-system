USAGE RULES
Gold (#C6A96B) — the most important rule
Use sparingly. One emphasis per section maximum.
Never use as a background colour.
It is punctuation, not decoration.
Its power comes from restraint. Overuse kills it.
Deep Plum (#190C19) — dark mode caution
Do not use as a full-page dark mode background in landing contexts.
Fariz feedback confirmed: heavy dark palette creates friction for pitch/landing use.
Reserve for slides, dark sections, and brand collateral.
Landing page default is always Warm White (#F8F6F7).
Plum Tint (#EDE6ED) — panel surfaces
Use at 60% opacity for frosted glass panel effect.
Pairs with backdrop-filter blur 8–12px.
Creates depth without heaviness.
Text hierarchy
Headlines and body: #190C19 (Deep Plum)
Supporting / secondary: #6B4D6B (Plum Mid)
Labels and overlines: #C6A96B (Muted Gold), Aileron, 11px, all caps
Never use opacity to de-emphasise — cut the copy instead.
What never happens
Gold as background
Pure black (#000000) or pure white (#FFFFFF) — use the brand versions
Any colour outside this palette without explicit approval
Gradient fills of any kind
